# Code of Conduct

CraftUI is a friendly project, and we want it to stay that way for everyone,
whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and assume good faith.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers, and remember that everyone started somewhere.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

This applies in every project space, including issues, pull requests and
discussions. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules.

To report a problem, use a
[private report](https://github.com/amblydia/craft-ui/security/advisories/new) or
contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled in confidence.
