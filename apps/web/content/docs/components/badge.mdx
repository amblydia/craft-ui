## Accessibility

- Every variant holds its text at 4.5:1 or more.
- A badge is plain text. Don't rely on its color alone: the words should say the status.

## Customization

The variants follow Ore UI's tags. In its names, `info` is Informative, `warning` is Notice, `destructive` is Warning and `accent` is Realms Informative.

To style a link as a badge, use `badgeVariants`:

```tsx
<a className={badgeVariants({ variant: "info" })} href="/news">
  New
</a>
```
